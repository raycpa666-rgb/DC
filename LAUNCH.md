# Launch plan: first $100

You need to do the steps that involve your identity or money: sign up, get paid, post. Allow about 2 hours of setup, then about 30 minutes a day.

## 1. Set up the store (20 min)
1. Create a free account at **gumroad.com**. It hosts the file, takes card payments and handles sales tax. Their fee is about 10% + $0.50 a sale, so you net about $7.60 from a $9 sale.
   Alternatives: Lemon Squeezy, Payhip, or Etsy (its "digital download" listings get search traffic from buyers).
2. New product → Digital product → upload `product/Budget-and-Debt-Payoff-Planner.xlsx`.
3. Price: **$9**. Copy the description from `docs/index.html`.
4. Cover image: `marketing/images/gumroad-cover-1280x720.png`. All listing and post text is in `marketing/COPY.md`.
5. Turn on "Allow customers to pay what they want" with a $9 minimum. Some people will pay more.

## 2. Publish the sales page (10 min)
1. In `docs/index.html`, replace `BUY_URL` with your Gumroad product link.
2. GitHub repo → Settings → Pages → Source: branch `main`, folder `/docs`. Save.
3. Your page will be at `https://<username>.github.io/<repo>/`.
   (You can skip this and share the Gumroad link directly. The page is optional.)

## 3. Get buyers (ongoing)
Where people already ask "how do I pay off debt faster":

| Channel | What to do | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| **Etsy** | List it as well. "Debt payoff tracker spreadsheet" is a well-searched category. | $0.20 listing fee |
| **Reddit** r/personalfinance, r/povertyfinance, r/debtfree | Answer real questions with the actual math ("at 23% APR an extra $100/mo saves you ~$1,700"). Link the planner only where self-promotion is allowed, e.g. weekly threads. | Read each sub's rules first. Spam gets you banned. |
| **Pinterest** | 5–10 pins from screenshots ("Debt-free date calculator"). Pins keep getting traffic for months. | Free |
| **TikTok / Reels / Shorts** | 15-second screen recording: type in a debt, add $100 extra, show the "interest saved" number. | Free |
| **Friends & family** | Text 20 people you know who are paying off debt. Honestly this is the fastest way to the first 5 sales. | Free |

Aim for: 5 sales from people you know, then the rest from Etsy plus 1–2 useful posts a day.

## 4. Track it
| Sales | Revenue (net) |
|---|---|
| 13 | ~$100 ✅ |

If you have 0 sales after 50+ page visits, change the headline or the cover image before you try new channels.
